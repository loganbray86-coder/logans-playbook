🔒 What this add-on does with your ESPN sign-in
  • When you tap Connect, it reads only two ESPN cookies, espn_s2 and SWID. It never sees your password.
  • It sends them once, over HTTPS, only to the Playbook server picked below. Playbook checks them with one read of your league and sends back a locked (encrypted) key for each league.
  • The add-on never saves your cookies. It keeps only the server you picked. No analytics, no tracking, nothing else.
  • Playbook only reads ESPN. It can't make moves or change your team. Signing out of ESPN everywhere turns the connection off.
